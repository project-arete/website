Project Arete Primer

A short introduction for readers who want the whole picture on one page.

What Is Arete?

Arete is an open, interoperable architecture that transforms connectivity from address-based models to role- and context-based orchestration of distributed digital systems. It is the practical realization of the CP Network vision, focusing on coordination, security, and composability through standardized Connection Profiles licensed openly via GitHub.

Core Architecture

  • Orchestrator Systems — the control plane, brokering and enforcing policies across constituent domains.
  • Constituent Systems — contribute Nodes: devices, digital assets, services, and other resources ready to be orchestrated.
  • Nodes — atomic units, each declaring roles and capabilities, synchronized between orchestrators and their original systems.
  • Connection Profiles (CPs) — contracts specifying role, terms, and properties of every interaction.
  • Contexts — group capabilities for semantic interoperability.

How Arete Works

  • Orchestrators maintain a synchronized inventory of Nodes.
  • Contexts are matched for orchestration.
  • Capabilities are paired through Connection Profiles.
  • Connections are continuously audited and governed under zero-trust models.

Example — a single building: Arete acts as the building’s single source of truth for operations, linking HVAC, lighting, security, BIM, CMMS, and more — plug-and-play interoperability and a trusted data fabric for every stakeholder.

Value Proposition

  • Interoperability — connects siloed systems, enabling new operating models.
  • Security — role- and context-based, with full auditability.
  • Composability — create and combine services without complex integration projects.
